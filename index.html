Data & Sources

  • 01–03 · Country participation: FIDE federation counts and World Bank 2025 population estimates. Rankings use the same 200 eligible federations; Taiwan is excluded for missing population data. Missing map values are not zero.
  • 04–05 · Rating comparisons: FIDE, October 2026; 266,109 rated records in the eight largest federations. Whiskers extend to observations within 1.5×IQR; unusual values remain in calculations.
  • 06 · Openings: Chess Tempo openings dataset via Kaggle. The chart uses 1,882 records with valid first-move notation, 100+ high-Elo games, and last-played dates from 2010–2018. Shares use summed source counts.
  • 07–08 · Ratings and ranks: Chart 07 includes 569,651 positive standard ratings, including FID and NON records; its 100-point bins are lower-inclusive and upper-exclusive. A 2200+ rating is not an official title. Chart 08 uses FIDE counts and World Bank 2025 population.
  • 09 · Elite player rankings: FIDE standard-rated player data, October 2026. The chart compares 25 selected federations using their number of players rated 2200 or above and the share of their standard-rated player populations. It is not a complete global ranking.
  • 10 · Age: 561,184 players have positive standard ratings and valid birth years; 8,467 rated records without a valid birth year are excluded. Age is estimated as 2026 minus birth year, so this is a cross-sectional comparison.
  • 11 · Time controls: 194,380 unique FIDE records have positive Standard, Rapid and Blitz ratings. The three pairwise matrices compare rating-group counts between controls; these bands are analytical, not official categories, and do not show change over time.
